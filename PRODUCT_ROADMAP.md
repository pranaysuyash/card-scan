# Quantum Card Scanner - Product Roadmap

## Vision
Build the most beautiful, intelligent, and privacy-focused business card scanner that transforms networking from a chore into a delightful experience.

## Core Principles
- **Privacy First** - Local-first, no cloud unless user wants it
- **Beautiful UX** - Every interaction should feel magical
- **Smart by Default** - AI that works for you, not against you
- **Professional Grade** - CRM-lite for solopreneurs and professionals

## Competitive Analysis

### vs CamCard
**Our Advantages:**
- ✅ Better design (quantum theme)
- ✅ Privacy-first (local-only)
- ✅ Free forever core features
- ✅ Open source potential
- ❌ Less mature AI
- ❌ No cloud sync yet

### vs LinkedIn Card Scanner
**Our Advantages:**
- ✅ Standalone app
- ✅ Not tied to LinkedIn
- ✅ More export options
- ✅ Better organization
- ❌ No automatic LinkedIn enrichment
- ❌ Smaller network effect

### vs Evernote Scannable
**Our Advantages:**
- ✅ Purpose-built for business cards
- ✅ Better contact management
- ✅ CRM-lite features
- ✅ Beautiful specialized UI
- ❌ Less document versatility
